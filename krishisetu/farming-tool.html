Know Your Farming Tools

🚜
FARM MACHINERY

Tractor

🎯
USED FOR

Used for pulling agricultural equipment and transporting loads.

📅
WHEN USED

Used during land preparation, sowing, harvesting and transport.

⚙️
PURPOSE

Reduces manual labour and saves time in farming.

🔩
LAND PREPARATION

Plough

🎯
USED FOR

Used to turn and loosen the soil before planting.

📅
WHEN USED

Mainly used before sowing during land preparation.

⚙️
PURPOSE

Helps control weeds and prepares the soil.

🌱
SOIL PREPARATION

Cultivator

🎯
USED FOR

Used to loosen soil and control weeds.

📅
WHEN USED

Used before planting or between crop rows.

⚙️
PURPOSE

Improves soil condition and removes weeds.

⚙️
LAND PREPARATION

Rotavator

🎯
USED FOR

Used to break and mix soil quickly.
