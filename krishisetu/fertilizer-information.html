FARMING INFORMATION

Know Your Fertilizers

Learn what each fertilizer is used for and when to apply it.
🌿
NITROGEN FERTILIZER

Urea

🎯
USED FOR

Provides nitrogen for green leaves and vegetative plant growth.

📅
WHEN TO USE

Used during the active growth stage according to crop requirements.

🌾
COMMON CROPS

Rice, wheat, maize and sugarcane.

💎
NITROGEN + PHOSPHORUS

DAP

🎯
USED FOR

Supports early root development and healthy plant establishment.

📅
WHEN TO USE

Commonly applied during sowing or before planting.

🌾
COMMON CROPS

Wheat, maize, pulses and oilseeds.

🌈
BALANCED NUTRIENTS

NPK Fertilizer

🎯
USED FOR

Provides nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium to plants.

📅
WHEN TO USE

Used according to crop stage and nutrient requirements.

🌾
COMMON CROPS

Vegetables, fruits and field crops.

🟣
POTASSIUM FERTILIZER

MOP

🎯
USED FOR

Helps improve crop quality and plant strength.

📅
WHEN TO USE

Applied according to soil and crop potassium requirements.

🌾
COMMON CROPS

Sugarcane, banana and potato.

⚪
PHOSPHORUS FERTILIZER

SSP

🎯
USED FOR

Supports root growth and flowering development.

📅
WHEN TO USE

Usually applied before or during sowing.

🌾
COMMON CROPS

Oilseeds, pulses and vegetables.
